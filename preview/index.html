I lead a research team at Apodex building AI that is both capable and verifiable. Mathematics is where reasoning ability is hardest to fake, and verification is what makes that ability dependable at scale — we work on both. Most of our current work is not yet public.

What we work on

  • Mathematical capabilities of LLMs. The full training stack — pre-training, mid-training, post-training, and agents — directed at models that can do mathematics.
  • Verification. Formal verification for LLM training infrastructure, and verifiers that make agents efficient and reliable on long-horizon tasks.

Background

Before Apodex, I was a research scientist at Meta FAIR in New York, a postdoctoral fellow at Caltech, and earned a PhD in computer science at Princeton. I have worked on machine learning for formal mathematics and theorem proving since 2018, and much of the open-source tooling my collaborators and I built is in regular use across that field.
