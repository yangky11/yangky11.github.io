Kaiyu Yang leads a research team at Apodex working on large language models for mathematics and verification. His research spans the full training stack for mathematical capability, as well as verifiers that make AI outputs dependable, from proofs and code to AI's own training pipelines. He has worked on machine learning for formal mathematics since 2018, building open-source tools such as LeanDojo that are in regular use in the field. Previously, he was a research scientist at Meta FAIR and a postdoctoral fellow at Caltech. He received a Ph.D. in computer science from Princeton University and bachelor's degrees from Tsinghua University.
