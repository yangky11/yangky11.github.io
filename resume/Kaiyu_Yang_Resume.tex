I lead a research team at Apodex working on large language models for mathematics and
verification. AI systems generate code and proofs faster than anyone can check them; as
generation gets cheaper, verification becomes the bottleneck. I have worked on machine
learning for formal mathematics since my PhD.

\section{Experience}

\entry{Apodex}{Redwood City, CA}{Lead Scientist}{Mar 2026 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Lead a team of five researchers on the mathematical capabilities of LLMs across the
        training stack: pre-training data that aims to capture all the math on the web,
        synthetic mid-training data distilled from research papers, and post-training
        pushing toward research-level mathematics.
  \item Build verifiers that help agents complete long-horizon tasks, applied to AI's own
        training pipelines, from the correctness of GPU kernels to the quality of training data.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.25em}
\entry{Meta Fundamental AI Research (FAIR)}{New York, NY}{Research Scientist}{Jun 2024 -- Dec 2025}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Advised Goedel-Prover and Goedel-Prover-V2 (COLM 2025, ICLR 2026), theorem provers
        that set the state of the art among open models at release, outperforming provers
        20$\times$ their size; V2 is downloaded about 10,000 times a month.
  \item Co-developed ProofOptimizer (ICLR 2026), a model that simplifies machine-generated
        proofs without human demonstrations; collaborated on verified code generation
        (Verina, ICLR 2026; early adopters include AWS and Harmonic).
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.25em}
\entry{California Institute of Technology}{Pasadena, CA}{Postdoctoral Fellow}{Sep 2022 -- May 2024}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built \href{https://www.leandojo.org/}{LeanDojo} (NeurIPS 2023), the first
        open-source infrastructure for theorem proving with LLMs in Lean, used by projects
        at Meta, ByteDance, and DeepSeek.
  \item Autoformalizing Euclidean Geometry (ICML 2024) enabled the first faithful
        formalization of Euclid's \textit{Elements}, Book I, in a modern proof assistant.
\end{itemize}
