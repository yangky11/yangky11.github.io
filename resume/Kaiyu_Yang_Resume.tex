I lead a research team at Apodex working on large language models for mathematics and
verification. AI systems generate code and proofs faster than anyone can check them; as
generation gets cheaper, verification becomes the bottleneck. I have worked on machine
learning for formal mathematics since my PhD.

\section{Experience}

\entry{Apodex}{Redwood City, CA}{Lead Scientist}{Mar 2026 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Lead research on the mathematical capabilities of LLMs across the training stack:
        pre-training data that aims to capture all the math on the web, synthetic
        mid-training data distilled from research papers, and post-training pushing toward
        research-level mathematics.
  \item Build verifiers that help agents complete long-horizon tasks, applied to AI's own
        training pipelines, from the correctness of GPU kernels to the quality of training data.
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}
\entry{Meta Fundamental AI Research (FAIR)}{New York, NY}{Research Scientist}{Jun 2024 -- Dec 2025}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Advised Goedel-Prover and Goedel-Prover-V2 (COLM 2025, ICLR 2026), theorem provers
        that set the state of the art among open models at release, outperforming provers
        20$\times$ their size; V2 is downloaded about 10,000 times a month.
  \item Worked on ProofOptimizer (ICLR 2026), a model that simplifies machine-generated
        proofs without human demonstrations; collaborations on verified code generation
        (Verina, ICLR 2026; early adopters include AWS and Harmonic) and neuro-symbolic
        olympiad problem solving (ICLR 2025, CAV 2025).
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.5em}
\entry{California Institute of Technology}{Pasadena, CA}{Postdoctoral Fellow}{Sep 2022 -- May 2024}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built \href{https://www.leandojo.org/}{LeanDojo} (NeurIPS 2023), the first
        open-source infrastructure for theorem proving with LLMs in Lean, used by projects
        at Meta, ByteDance, and DeepSeek; and Lean Copilot, which runs LLM inference
        natively inside the Lean proof assistant.
  \item Autoformalizing Euclidean Geometry (ICML 2024) enabled the first faithful
        formalization of Euclid's \textit{Elements}, Book I, in a modern proof assistant.
\end{itemize}

\section{Open Source}

Core developer of open-source tooling for machine learning with the Lean and Coq proof
assistants:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \href{https://github.com/lean-dojo/LeanDojo}{LeanDojo} --- interacting with Lean
        from machine-learning pipelines; 800+ GitHub stars.
  \item \href{https://github.com/lean-dojo/ReProver}{ReProver} --- retrieval-augmented
        theorem prover; 300+ stars.
  \item \href{https://github.com/lean-dojo/LeanCopilot}{Lean Copilot} --- LLM inference
        inside Lean for proof automation; 1,300+ stars.
  \item \href{https://github.com/princeton-vl/CoqGym}{CoqGym} --- learning environment for
        the Coq proof assistant; 400+ stars.
  \item \href{https://huggingface.co/collections/kaiyuy/leandojo}{LeanDojo models} on Hugging Face ---
        retrieval and tactic-generation models; about 8,000 downloads a month.
\end{itemize}
